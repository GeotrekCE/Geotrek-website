<p>Created in 2018 and designed by the <strong>LPO Is&egrave;re</strong>, this module allows:</p>
<ul>
    <li>to <strong>finely manage</strong> regulatory zones and sensitive zones in Geotrek,&nbsp;</li>
    <li>to <strong>provide information</strong> on the areas of biotope protection orders: location, regulation period, associated description, species, typology of practice, etc.,</li>
    <li>to <strong>automatically connect</strong> all nearby Geotrek objects..&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
<a href="https://biodiv-sports.fr/" target="_blank"><img style="max-width: 100%;" src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2024/biodivsport.jpeg"></a>
<!--more-->
<p><strong>What are the advantages?</strong></p>
<p>When using Geotrek to navigate a route, the user is alerted if an environmental sensitivity area is nearby, informing them of the associated regulations.<br><br>This project aligns with the awareness and information objective that Geotrek wishes to achieve, a crucial issue in a context of increasing pressure on Biodiversity 🌳 🐦.</p>

Over time, the <strong>Sensitivity module</strong> has become a national platform :&nbsp;<strong>Biodiv&rsquo;Sport</strong>. You can enter sensitivity data on <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://biodiv-sports.fr/">https://biodiv-sports.fr/</a>, so that they are reported in any Geotrek, but also on other platforms:&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.camptocamp.org/">Camptocamp</a>,&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.visorando.com/">Visorando</a>,&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://georiviere.fr/">GeoRivi&egrave;re</a>, etc.&nbsp;&nbsp;<br><br><strong>Biodiv&rsquo;Sport</strong> and the <strong>Sensitivity module</strong> perfectly illustrate collaboration between territories and the centralization of information in the service of biodiversity and public awareness.&nbsp;<br>It is also an example of the growing importance of an ecosystem of interconnected solutions, in which <strong>Geotrek</strong> plays an increasingly central role.<br>&nbsp;
