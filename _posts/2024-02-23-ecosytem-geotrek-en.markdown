<p><b>What if we told you that entering a hike just once so that it can then be broadcast on Cirkwi, IGNrando', Apidae, Visorando or Outdooractive is possible?<br>Welcome to the Geotrek ecosystem!</b>

<br><br>For several years, Geotrek has established itself in France as the tool favored by administrative structures for the management of their territory. However, you will surely have noticed that there are more and more tools to promote itineraries on the internet.<br><b>Faced with this observation, a philosophy was quickly chosen: never having to enter information twice anywhere.</b><br><br>Thanks to its public data interface, many development tools have learned to read and integrate data from Geotrek:</p>
<ul>
    <li>Apidae</li>
    <li>VisoRando</li>
    <li>IGN Rando</li>
    <li>OutdoorActive</li>
    <li>Cirkwi</li>
    <li>Kalkin</li>
    <li>Suricate<br>&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
<img style="max-width: 70%;"
    alt="Widget Geotrek"
    src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2024/ecosystem_geotrek_2.jpeg">
<p>Why distribute data to other platforms? Visitors and users already have online browsing habits and will not have the reflex to go to your website anyway. What matters is that <b>no matter their preferred website, they can access YOUR quality information</b>, written thanks to your knowledge of local issues, and thus do not risk going to unwanted places for various issues (danger, trail closure, conflict of use, etc.).<br><br>It's nice to export data to these tools, but can I import my content that already exists elsewhere?<b>Of course, Geotrek's strength is also being able to communicate with these platforms to import content.</b> So, if you want to benefit from up-to-date content synchronized with the following platforms, it is possible:</p>
<ul>
    <li>Tourinsoft</li>
    <li>Apidae</li>
    <li>LEI</li>
    <li>Biodiv’Sport</li>
    <li>IGNrando'</li>
    <li>Cirkwi</li>
    <li>Visorando</li>
    <li>Outdooractive</li>
    <li>Esprit Parc National</li>
    <li>Suricate<br>&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Geotrek is the solution for interconnecting an entire ecosystem of tools linked to the world of outdoor activities.</b></p>
